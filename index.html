Our mission

Nature immersion that sticks with kids.

Sprouts & Wings is a warm, after‑school club where children slow down, notice more, and build care for the living world. Sessions blend movement, observation, and creative making.

  • Guided by educators and local naturalists
  • Hands‑on skills: planting, bird ID, journaling
  • Always outdoors, rain or shine
